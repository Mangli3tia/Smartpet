Camera Detection Settings

Configure snapshot analysis parameters

Demo Mode
30%

Probability of generating a random camera motion alert on each snapshot. Higher values produce more frequent test alerts. Only applies to Demo Mode.

0–1

How similar two consecutive snapshots must be to count as no movement. Higher values mean even tiny changes are treated as movement. Lower values are more tolerant of noise.

frames

Number of consecutive no-change snapshots before a pet-inactivity alert is triggered. Higher values mean you wait longer before being notified.

Alert Thresholds

Configure temperature & humidity alert limits

°C

Maximum safe ambient temperature. When exceeded, a high-temperature alert is triggered to warn you that the environment may be too hot for your pet.

%

Minimum safe relative humidity. When humidity drops below this value, a low-humidity alert is triggered to warn you that the air may be too dry for your pet.
